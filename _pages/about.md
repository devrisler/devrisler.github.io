Hello there! I am Devriş İşler and a recent PhD graduate.

My research has focused on developing trustworthy and verifiable AI and data-driven systems through applied cryptography, secure systems, and privacy enhancing technologies. Throughout my PhD, I investigated how provenance, ownership, accountability, authenticity, and privacy can be established and independently verified in modern data and machine-learning ecosystems. I am interested in trustworthy AI, including auditing, accountability, and governance. With the advancement of AI in recent years, I believe there is a growing need for mechanisms that support reliable monitoring, verification, and oversight of AI systems. While my expertise lies in applied cryptography and secure systems design, I value interdisciplinary research that connects technical guarantees with governance, human-centered design, and policy. My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of safe, transparent, privacy-preserving, and accountable AI systems, while helping bridge the gap between technical solutions, real-world deployment, and regulatory frameworks.
